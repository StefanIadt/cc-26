# What IADT graduates make

There is a type of computing graduate who can build something and also explain
why it should exist, how it should feel, and who it is for. That is what we
are trying to produce here.

## Strengths

- Students learn to design and code from the beginning – neither is an afterthought
- Projects are real, deployed, and defended – not just submitted
- Every project is presented and critiqued – communication is part of the work, not separate from it
- The institution sits inside an arts and design culture – that changes how students think about what they make
- Graduates leave with a portfolio of work that reflects genuine ambition, not just technical competence
