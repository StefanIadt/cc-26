# A degree that already works

This is not a proposal for something new.
It is a proposal to be honest about what already exists —
and to build a structure around it that makes it consistent,
repeatable and legible.

## What we already know

– Some of our students make things that are genuinely impressive.
  Not impressive for 250 CAO points. Impressive full stop.

– The modules where this happens share something in common.
  They are sprint-based. Students make something, show it,
  get honest feedback, and make it again.

– This pedagogy is not consistent across the programme.
  It depends on which lecturer a student happens to have.
  The proposal is to make it the spine, not the exception.

– We are not a design school. We are not a computer science school.
  We are something rarer — a place where developers are expected
  to care about what they make, not just whether it works.

## What the research will tell us

A programme review is currently underway. Research is being conducted
with prospective students, parents, FE colleges, industry partners
and current students between now and October 2026.

The argument on this page is provisional. It is the team's current
thinking — not the conclusion. The research will challenge, refine
and in some cases contradict what is written here.

That is the point.

## What we are proposing

A four-year sprint-based degree with a single spine and two streams.

**The spine – Years 1 and 2**

All students. Same experience. Sprint rhythm from week one.
Students make something every few weeks, show it, defend it, improve it.
The first semester involves a Figma interface and a working game.
Not theory. Not slides. Things.

By the end of Year 2, students know which direction they lean.
The structure acknowledges that and gives it a name.

**The streams – Years 3 and 4**

– Frontend + Experience
  Interfaces, interactions, immersive technology, physical computing,
  data visualisation. Students who want to make things that are
  considered, crafted and visually alive.

– Backend + Systems
  Full-stack architecture, DevOps, applied AI, engineering.
  Students who want to make things that are robust, scalable
  and technically serious.

Both streams use the same sprint structure.
Both streams do crits. Both streams present and defend their work.
The difference is in the specialism, not the culture.

**Year 4 – the major project**

Already sprint-based. Already working.
Students produce one significant piece of work.
It reflects four years, not four weeks.

The On Show exhibition is not a bolt-on event.
It is the final sprint review.

## What makes this IADT

The institution.

Students spend four years in a building that also houses
film, animation, graphic design, photography and UX design.
That is not incidental. It changes how they think about
what they make.

No other Irish computing degree has that context.
Most do not think it matters.
We think it is the whole point.
